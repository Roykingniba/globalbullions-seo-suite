Connecting a WordPress site

Connection uses a WordPress Application Password, not your normal login. It is a separate credential you can revoke at any time without changing your password, and it works on WordPress 5.6 and later.

  1. Sign in to your WordPress admin and open Users, then your profile.
  2. Scroll to Application Passwords, enter a name such as "Kai SEO Suite", and click Add.
  3. Copy the password WordPress shows you. It is displayed once and never again.
  4. Paste it here along with your site address and WordPress username.

The application password is encrypted before it is stored, with the key held outside the database. If you ever want to cut access, delete the entry in WordPress and it stops working immediately.
