Why placeholder text is nonsense on purpose

Lorem ipsum is scrambled Latin, so it has no meaning to distract you. When you fill a mockup with real, readable copy, people start reviewing the writing instead of the layout. Nonsense text keeps the conversation on spacing, line length and hierarchy, which is what a mockup is for.

It also roughly matches the letter and word length distribution of English, so a block of it takes up about as much room as the real copy eventually will.

One warning. Never let placeholder text reach a live page. Search engines will index it, and pages full of lorem ipsum look abandoned to anyone who lands on them. Search your site for "lorem" before you launch.
