Which format to use where

  • HEX is the most common in design handoffs and CSS. Compact but hard to adjust by eye.
  • RGB maps directly to how a screen mixes light. Useful when you need a specific channel value.
  • HSL is the easiest to reason about. Hue is the colour, saturation is how vivid it is, and lightness is how bright. To build a hover state, keep hue and saturation and drop the lightness by ten percent.
