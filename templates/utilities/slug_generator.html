What makes a good slug

  • Use hyphens between words. Search engines read hyphens as spaces, underscores less reliably.
  • Keep it short and readable. Around 3 to 5 words is plenty, and a slug someone can read aloud is easier to share.
  • Drop filler words like "a", "the" and "of" when they add nothing.
  • Accented letters are converted to their plain equivalents, so "Crème Brûlée" becomes "creme-brulee" instead of losing those words entirely.
  • Once a page is live, changing its slug changes its URL. Set up a redirect from the old address or you will lose the traffic and links pointing at it.
