Why consistency matters more than the tags themselves

Analytics tools treat tag values as literal text. Facebook, facebook and FaceBook become three separate rows in your report, splitting one campaign across three lines and making the numbers look smaller than they are. That is why the lowercase option is on by default.

Spaces cause the same problem, so any space you type is turned into a hyphen.

Agree on a naming convention before you start tagging, write it down, and stick to it. Most messy analytics data comes from three people inventing three different spellings of the same campaign.

One thing to avoid: never put UTM tags on internal links between pages of your own site. Doing so restarts the visitor's session and overwrites the original source, so you lose the record of how they actually found you.
